shortBallot

Javascript, Web

Javascript, Web

64% of high school/college students do not vote or register to vote here in Indiana. Our project, shortBallot, aims to fix this problem in our community by providing a simplified, unbiased version of our local ballot (Hamilton County), with the goal of increasing voter turnout in our school and among our peers.

to quote from our CSForGood submission: "Our project, shortBallot, aims to create a “one-stop-shop” of sorts that provides users of Hamilton County a simplified version of the upcoming ballot, with “cards” of the candidates that can be clicked and display information about each candidate. In practice, it would be a situational application that the user would open near election day or the voter registration deadline and receive all their information right on the app, without the need to do individual research."

pretty cool stuff!!! : D


While it was a group project, most of my responsibilities were on the CSS side of things, as well as the HTML structure of the main page! This includes the layout of the cards, the way the page came across, as well as some additional research on each candidate and their roles.


While it is hard to give an exact estimate for my timeline, I spent the first half of our allotted worktime working on the CSS and backbone of the site, while i spent around the last week or so(?) working on researching each election in our county as well as who was running in it! <3
